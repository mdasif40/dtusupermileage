Who We Are

DTU Supermileage, founded in 2005, is a collegiate research team of young car enthusiasts from Delhi Technological University with the goal of designing and building high-mileage and super efficient gasoline, electric & Solar vehicles while keeping the impending fuel crisis in mind.

To accomplish this, we design, manufacture and advertise innovative products and systems that reduce our environmental impact, increase productivity, improve energy efficiency and ensure the safety of our communities.

ELECTRICAL AND EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

This department oversees the production of powertrain components, electric vehicles, and the effective operation of electronics, motor, and motor control systems.

AERODYNAMICS

Focuses on vehicle chassis design, structural optimization, lightweight material implementation, and reducing aerodynamic drag coefficient.

PROPULSION & VEHICLE DYNAMICS

Responsible for suspension geometry, steering systems, braking setups, and rigorous testing on track handling metrics.

STEERING AND BRAKES

Handles precision steering feedback systems and reliable braking mechanics tailored for maximum efficiency.

CORPORATE & DESIGN

Handles corporate partnerships, financial budgeting, logistical planning, and overall outreach for national and international events.
