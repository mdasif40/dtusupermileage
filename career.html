Career

Delhi Technological University

The Delhi Technological University (Formerly Delhi College of Engineering) has provided us with space to set up our lab and work day and night without disturbance at the Wind Tunnel area. The Team operates entirely from the lab space provided and gets a lot of support from University.

The university assists us by providing a Faculty advisor who clears up our doubts and provides valuable insights based on their extensive knowledge and experience. In addition to technical assistance, we receive Financial assistance from the University, which aids in the manufacture of the vehicle.

OUR DEPARTMENTS

PROPULSION & VEHICLE DYNAMICS

Optimizes vehicle handling, stability, and grip by engineering the suspension geometry, managing weight distribution, and controlling overall vehicle behavior through simulation and track testing.

STEERING & BRAKES

The department plans the steering structure and considers how the brake system operates. They are in charge of the car's handling and safety

AERODYNAMICS

Shapes the vehicle's body shell and fairings to minimize drag while balancing downforce and cooling. Using CFD and testing, they optimize overall efficiency and collaborate with mechanical teams to integrate airflow with the car's layout

ELECTRICAL AND EMBEDDED

Designs the vehicle's custom wiring harnesses, power distribution systems, and microcontrollers. Using simulation tools and rigorous testing, they manage reliable power flow and data communication across all onboard sensors and control units

CORPORATE AND DESIGN

Manages team branding, social media presence, and visual identity to attract sponsors and engage the public. They secure vital financial partnerships through professional proposals, pitches, and stakeholder relations.
